# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

During this laboratory activity, I learned more about AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I chose GCP because it offers useful services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Kubernetes, and application development. It also provides resources that can help businesses grow.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide services for virtual machines, storage, databases, security, networking, and Kubernetes. They also use a pay-as-you-go model, where users pay for the resources they use. Although their service names are different, many of their services perform similar tasks.

I learned that business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform. For example, a startup with a limited budget may need affordable and flexible services. A university that already uses Microsoft products may prefer Azure. A company focused on AI and Machine Learning may choose GCP. A large global company may choose AWS because of its wide range of services and scalability.

Working as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to understand a client's needs before choosing a cloud platform. I learned that there is no single cloud provider that is best for everyone. The best choice depends on the organization's budget, technology, performance, security, and future goals.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved during this mission. I added cloud research, comparison tables, decision matrices, Linux investigation results, and client recommendations. These activities helped me organize my work and understand cloud computing better. I also learned how to explain technical topics in a simple and easy-to-understand way.
